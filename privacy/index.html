Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

dpuig Publisher ("the App") is a personal tool operated by Daniel Puig Gerarde to publish videos to the operator's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

Information we access

When the account owner authorizes the App via TikTok Login Kit, the App receives an OAuth access token and basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and, with the granted scopes, the ability to upload and publish videos to that account.

How we use it

Data is used solely to upload and publish content the account owner chooses. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Access and refresh tokens are stored securely by the operator and deleted when access is revoked. No other TikTok user data is stored.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
